About Us

Safe Savings, Secure Future.

Shree Capital Credit Co-operative Society Ltd. is a Jodhpur-based credit co-operative registered under the Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act. We exist for one reason — to help our members build a strong financial foundation for themselves and the people they love.

Members deposit their savings with us through Fixed Deposits, the Monthly Income Scheme and Recurring Deposits. Those savings are lent back to the community as Group and Individual loans. Every rupee stays accounted for.

  • Registered co-operative society, governed by its members
  • Transparent rate card with no hidden charges
  • Personalised service from a team you can meet in person

  1. 1

    Become a member

    Fill a short form and pay the one-time membership share amount.

  2. 2

    Choose your product

    Pick a deposit scheme or a loan and settle on tenure and amount.

  3. 3

    Submit documents

    Hand over the five standard KYC documents listed alongside.

  4. 4

    Get confirmed

    Receive your deposit receipt or loan sanction, usually within a week.

Documents Required

  • PAN Card
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Address Proof

Both take a lump sum deposit. An FD compounds the interest and pays everything at maturity, so your money grows faster. MIS pays the interest out to you every month as income and returns the principal at maturity. Choose FD to grow wealth, MIS to draw regular income.

Five to twenty members form a group and stand as joint guarantors for each other. No physical collateral is needed. Our field officer verifies the group, sanctions individual limits within it, and collects repayments weekly, fortnightly or monthly. A clean repayment record raises the limit for the next cycle.

Group loans are typically sanctioned in three to seven working days after group verification. Individual loans depend on the amount and documents, and a loan against your own FD can often be disbursed the same day.
